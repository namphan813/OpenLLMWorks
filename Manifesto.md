# OpenLLMBench Manifesto

## Why We Exist

OpenLLMBench began as a simple weekend project.

The original goal was straightforward:
create a better way to organize and compare local Large Language Model benchmark results.

As we built the parser, database, and analytics engine, something became clear.

We weren't just building another benchmark tool.

We were building a historical record.

Every benchmark submission tells a story.

A new GPU.
A faster inference engine.
A software optimization.
A driver update.
A better compiler.
A community member experimenting on a Saturday afternoon.

Those moments deserve to be preserved.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple.

Build the most trusted, transparent, and historically accurate benchmark repository for local AI inference.

We believe benchmark history is just as valuable as benchmark results.

Today's fastest hardware will eventually become tomorrow's history.

We want that history to remain available for everyone.

---

## What We Believe

We believe benchmarking should be:

• Open

• Transparent

• Reproducible

• Community-driven

• Historically accurate

Technology moves quickly.

History disappears even faster.

OpenLLMBench exists to make sure it doesn't.

---

## Our Principles

We value:

Data integrity over convenience.

Verification over assumptions.

Long-term maintainability over short-term shortcuts.

Community collaboration over individual ownership.

Thoughtful engineering over rushed features.

We will never knowingly invent benchmark data.

We will always strive to preserve it.

---

## Local AI Matters

The future of AI will not exist only in cloud data centers.

It will also exist:

on desktops

on laptops

on home servers

on workstations

on devices we haven't imagined yet.

Local AI deserves professional benchmarking tools.

OpenLLMBench is our contribution to that future.

---

## Community

OpenLLMBench belongs to its community.

Whether you contribute code,
benchmark results,
documentation,
bug reports,
or ideas—

you are helping build something larger than any individual contributor.

Great open-source projects are built together.

We intend to keep it that way.

---

## Looking Forward

Our goal is not to become the biggest benchmark repository.

Our goal is to become the most trusted.

Trust is earned slowly.

One benchmark.

One contribution.

One improvement at a time.

---

Measure.

Understand.

Preserve.
