# The Founding Story of OpenLLMWorks

## It Started With a Simple Idea

OpenLLMWorks didn't begin with a business plan.

It didn't begin with investors.

It didn't begin with the goal of becoming the largest benchmark repository.

It began with curiosity.

The original idea was simple:

> Could we build a better way to collect, organize, and understand local LLM benchmark results?

As local AI continued to evolve, benchmark results were being shared everywhere—GitHub repositories, Reddit posts, Discord servers, blog articles, screenshots, and personal spreadsheets.

The information was valuable.

But it was scattered.

---

## Building the Foundation

The first weekends focused on solving technical problems.

We built a parser.

Then a database.

Then analytics.

Then snapshots.

Then leaderboards.

Then historical trends.

Each feature answered a question we wished someone else had already answered.

As the project grew, another realization emerged.

We weren't just collecting benchmark numbers.

We were documenting progress.

---

## A Different Way to Think About Benchmarks

Every benchmark represents a moment in time.

Someone upgraded a GPU.

Someone compiled a faster version of llama.cpp.

Someone discovered a new optimization.

Someone tested hardware that nobody else had documented yet.

Those moments matter.

Individually, they seem small.

Together, they tell the story of how local AI has evolved.

OpenLLMWorks exists to preserve that story.

---

## Designing for the Long Term

As the project matured, priorities shifted.

Instead of asking:

> "How do we add another feature?"

We started asking:

> "How do we make sure this project is still trustworthy five years from now?"

That change influenced everything.

Schema evolution.

Verified backups.

Historical snapshots.

Trend analytics.

Operations documentation.

Every addition was designed with longevity in mind.

---

## Community First

OpenLLMWorks is an open-source project because we believe benchmark history belongs to everyone.

The project will always value thoughtful contributions over rapid growth.

Whether someone contributes:

- a benchmark,
- documentation,
- code,
- testing,
- or simply an idea,

they become part of the project's story.

---

## Looking Ahead

There will always be faster hardware.

There will always be better inference engines.

There will always be another benchmark to run.

Our goal isn't to chase every new result.

Our goal is to preserve the journey.

Because today's benchmark becomes tomorrow's history.

---

Understand.

Preserve.
